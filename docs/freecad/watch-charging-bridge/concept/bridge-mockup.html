Watch Charging Bridge

A humpback stone bridge that doubles as a charging stand. The B9 watch body nests face-up in a curved cradle on the crown with its back on the magnetic charger, and the mesh strap runs down both ramps between 3 mm parapet walls. The charging cable drops through the deck into the arch and exits out the back.

Changes since rev A

  • The watch body is 19.98 mm wide, so it now sits between the walls. The walls run the full length and there's no flat landing any more.
  • The crown is curved to match the watch's curved back, so the body nests in it instead of rocking on a flat top.
  • Over the 50 mm where the watch body sits, the channel widens slightly to 20.6 mm. A 19.98 mm body in a 20.0 mm printed channel would jam. The strap channel on the ramps stays at 20 mm.
  • The charger has moved to the charging end of the watch, 14.6 mm from the middle. It's tilted 13.2° so it sits square to the curved crown. The watch goes on one way round, with the CE end toward the other ramp.
  • Rev C: uses your measured 4.1 mm thickness and 8 mm contact position. The crown curve is now R64 and the charger sits 14.6 mm off centre.
